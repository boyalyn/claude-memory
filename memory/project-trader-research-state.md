Findings so far (details in docs/*.md, plain summaries in docs/README.md):
- Form 4 insider cluster buys (SEC bulk data 2016-2026): unconditional rule fails; ML selection weak; best combo
  (walk-forward model + 20% take-profit + equal slots, idle cash in QQQ) ~+3-7%/yr over QQQ but t~1, ~15 variants
  tried; hypothesis for forward paper test only. Sell-exit, entry delay, sizing, signal-definition variants: no help.
  Grid search (180 combos) + permutation null: best +11.8%/yr vs QQQ (K=5, TP20) but reality-check p=0.06 (borderline); needs forward test.
- Form 4 'crash context + insider cluster buy' (ret120<=-40%): vs same-date crashed stocks w/o insider buying: +6.1% (t 1.8) overall, 2016-21 -4.4%, 2022+ +13.2%; portfolio K=3-10 beat QQQ by 4-12%/yr (t<1) but maxDD -59..-67%. Regime-dependent, unproven; best lead for a forward test (docs/form4-study.md Part V).
- Siblings scan (docs/form4-regime-and-scan.md): large/opportunistic/CEO-CFO insider buys in crashed (ret120<=-40%) or deep-drawdown (>=50% below 1y high) stocks beat same-state controls by ~6-7% over 60d, both halves, passes luck test; works in nearly all environments (10/11 years). BUT as a $25k portfolio vs QQQ: -7%..+3.7%/yr (t<1), maxDD -46..-75%. Real selector within crashed stocks, not a QQQ-beater.
- Health filter (docs/form4-health-filter.md): signal (ii) [insider buy >=$250k & ret120<=-40%] restricted to financially healthy stocks (cash runway>=2y or OCF>=0, liabilities<=80% assets, equity>0; SEC XBRL frames with +75/+100-day lags, financials excluded): K=10 +6.3%/yr vs QQQ, maxDD -35.7% (QQQ -35.1%), both halves positive, null percentile 96-99; monthly t only 1.4-1.6. Deep-drawdown version (iii) fails. Sensitivity (lag +30d, runway 1y/3y, lev 0.7/0.9): 5 of 6 pass the frozen bar (runway 1y just fails on maxDD -45.3%) => robust, minimum margin. KEY CAVEAT: vs QQQ the edge exists only for entries when QQQ is >=15% below its high (60 trades, +19.7% excess, alpha +10.7% t 2.2, beta 1.76) from just 4 selloff episodes (Dec18, Mar-Apr20, 2022, Apr25); near QQQ highs excess -0.7%. Next step planned: replicate on 2011-2015 (untouched, includes Aug 2011 / Aug 2015 selloffs). Best candidate for forward paper test; only ~13-30% of capital in stocks (rest QQQ).
- Random (naive) train/test splits inflate the Form 4 edge ~2x vs blocked-by-quarter/leave-one-year-out splits; always split by time blocks with embargo.
- Adding to QQQ in drawdowns >=15% (docs/qqq-dip-add-study.md): leverage 1.5x +0.7%/yr (2016-26) but maxDD -46%; reserve 20/40% costs 3-6%/yr; fails 1999-2026 (bear markets 2000/2008/2022 keep falling). The healthy-crash overlay beat all of these in 2016-26 (+4.6..6.3%, maxDD ~-31..-36%) but rests on 4 selloffs.
- Buying mid-cap dips (>=40% off 1y high): no edge, slightly worse than control, worse vs QQQ.
- IPO calendar: no usable buy point; buying ~day 25 after IPO lost to ordinary stocks.
- Price factors in $1-10B (momentum/low-vol/reversal): none beat QQQ.
- QQQ trend rules (200d, 10-month, 12m momentum): halve drawdown, lower return, no better Sharpe.
